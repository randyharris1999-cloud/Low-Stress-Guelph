        How the ratings work

        Each street is given a Level of Traffic Stress from 1 to 4, using the method planners use across North America. It considers the speed limit, the number of lanes, the type of street and the kind of bike facility, from paint to curbs.

        Trails and multi-use paths are level 1. A quiet 40 km/h residential street is level 1. Painted bike lanes on two-lane roads are level 2. Busier roads without protection are level 3 or 4. Crossing a busy road where there's no traffic signal is flagged as a stress point.

        Hills come from Ontario's LIDAR ground model, measured every half metre, so short steep blocks show up. It was flown in 2018, so streets graded since then may differ. Ratings are estimates from City data. Always ride to the conditions you find.

        Data: City of Guelph Open Data (street centrelines, bike facilities, trails, intersections, addresses, buildings, parks, watercourses) · Elevation: Ontario Digital Terrain Model (LIDAR-derived), Ontario Ministry of Natural Resources.
